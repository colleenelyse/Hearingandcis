Welcome. What sound would you like to experience?

Listen the way someone with a hearing loss or a cochlear implant might hear. Each simulator comes in two sizes.

Lite

Press play and try the buttons. Nothing to set up, very little reading.

Hearing loss LiteHear a story with mild to profound hearing loss, on an audiogram. Cochlear implant LiteHear an implant, and how it changes from 1 electrode to 22.

Full

Everything in Lite, plus your own files, YouTube, the microphone, every control and the background reading.

Hearing loss FullEach ear, noise, tinnitus, a hearing aid, and a mystery game. Cochlear implant FullHow implants work, surgery, timing, mapping, and Deaf culture.

Before you start

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a computer or Chromebook. The Full pages need it for YouTube.
  • Headphones give the best sound, and are needed for anything that treats each ear differently.
  • Every page has built-in recordings, so you can start listening right away. The Full pages also let you connect a YouTube video, upload your own audio or video files, or use the microphone.
