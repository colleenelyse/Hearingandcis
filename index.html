Welcome. What sound would you like to experience?

Listen to the world the way someone with a hearing loss, or a cochlear implant, might hear it. Each simulator comes in two sizes. Pick one to begin; you can switch at any time.

Lite

Press play and try the buttons. Built-in recordings only, nothing to set up, and very little reading. About two minutes.

Hearing loss Lite

Hear a story the way someone with mild, moderate, severe or profound hearing loss would, and see it on an audiogram.

Full

Before you start

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a computer or Chromebook. The Full pages need it for YouTube.
  • Headphones give the best sound, and are needed for anything that treats each ear differently.
  • Every page has built-in recordings, so you can start listening right away. The Full pages also let you connect a YouTube video, upload your own audio or video files, or use the microphone.
