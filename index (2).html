Welcome. What sound would you like to experience?

These two pages let you listen to the world the way someone with a hearing loss, or a cochlear implant, might hear it. Pick one to begin. You can switch to the other at any time.

Hearing loss simulator

Slide the faders on a real audiogram chart and hear what mild, moderate, severe and profound hearing loss do to speech and everyday sounds.

  • Common types of hearing loss, one click each
  • A different loss in each ear, with headphones
  • A mystery game: guess the hidden hearing loss
Open the hearing loss simulator

Cochlear implant simulator

Hear what a cochlear implant can sound like, switch electrodes on and off, and learn how implants work and who they are for.

  • Click each electrode to hear its pitch
  • How implants changed from 1 electrode to 22
  • Surgery, timing, mapping, and Deaf culture
Open the cochlear implant simulator

Before you start

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a computer or Chromebook.
  • Headphones give the best sound, and are needed for anything that treats each ear differently.
  • Each page has built-in recordings, so you can start listening right away.
